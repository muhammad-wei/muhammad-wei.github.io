  • The Convergence

    Where the four meet

    These are not four separate bets. AI is becoming an energy business; energy is becoming a capital and coordination business; space is becoming a compute and sensing business; and Web3 is the settlement and ownership layer that lets capital move across all three. The companies worth building sit at the seams — not inside a single industry.
